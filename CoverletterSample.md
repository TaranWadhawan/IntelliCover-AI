### Professional Background

Notably, I architected a hierarchical revenue system for an MLM client, reducing traversal complexity from O(n²) to O(n log n) and boosting calculation speed by 40%. This project not only optimized the system’s performance but also ensured data integrity for over 500 active users.

One of my key achievements is the development of an AI-native e-commerce microservices platform called Aurkira. This platform leveraged Java Spring Boot, Apache Kafka, and Redis to create a context-aware sales agent that uses WebSockets to track live user behavior and provide personalized recommendations. Additionally, I integrated Redis for caching, which reduced database read operations by 60%, further enhancing the system’s performance.

In the realm of GenAI, I developed Fino-Add-Wise, a financial assistant that democratizes access to expert advice using Python, FastAPI, and LangChain. This platform not only addresses the “financial labyrinth” but also includes a document deciphering feature that parses dense documents to flag hidden risks or non-compliant clauses. These projects have not only demonstrated my technical skills but also my ability to solve complex problems with innovative solutions.

# Best Format for the cover letter 


Dear Hiring Team,

I am a Computer Science undergraduate specializing in AI and Machine Learning, with a strong focus on building production-ready software and GenAI systems. I am applying for the Engineering Intern role because your work on AI-native financial workflows closely matches the systems I have already built and the direction I want to grow in.

Most of my work has been around designing real AI systems, not just experiments. In **FinoAddWise**, a GenAI-powered financial intelligence platform built with Python and FastAPI, I developed high-accuracy RAG pipelines for understanding insurance and loan documents. I worked on embeddings, retrieval logic, prompt tuning, and API orchestration to deliver reliable outputs for real financial use cases.

I have also built **agent-based systems** that coordinate complex workflows. In **MedhaSetu**, I designed autonomous backend agents with role-based access control, real-time state handling, and clean service boundaries. This project taught me how to think in terms of interfaces, ownership, and system behavior in production.

On the systems side, my flagship backend project **Aurkira** demonstrates my depth in production engineering. It is a Java Spring Boot microservices platform using Kafka, Redis, WebSockets, and event-driven architecture. I focused on clean abstractions, scalable APIs, caching strategies, and real-time communication. This experience helped me understand how large systems evolve and how to design for scale from day one.

I am comfortable working across the stack. I have built user-facing interfaces using React, Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. I focus on translating complex technical and financial logic into simple, intuitive flows that users can actually work with.

My work has also been recognized at a competitive level. I have won multiple national and global hackathons, **all for GenAI-driven systems**. This includes **Global Rank 1 at the Tencent Global Hackathon**, where I built a large-scale GenAI platform focused on real-world public impact. I have also won top positions at IIIT Hyderabad, IIT Bombay Techfest, IIT BHU, and other national competitions, often receiving awards for **Best Innovation, Best Idea, and Best Code**. These experiences strengthened my ability to build quickly while maintaining engineering quality.

I enjoy working in fast-moving startup environments where learning happens by building. I am comfortable asking questions, iterating fast, and taking increasing ownership as trust grows. I care deeply about clean code, strong fundamentals, and systems that actually ship to users.

I would love the opportunity to contribute to your team, learn from experienced engineers, and help build intelligent, agent-driven financial systems in production.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
**Ramharsh Sanjay Dandekar**
